Safety Protocols

Critical emergency response sequences for high-performance maritime operations. Immediate action is required for all active alerts.

MAYDAY

High Priority
01

Tune VHF Radio to Channel 16.

02

State "MAYDAY, MAYDAY, MAYDAY" clearly.

03

Identify vessel name and current coordinates from the display.

04

State nature of distress and number of persons on board.

05

Activate Emergency Position Indicating Radio Beacon (EPIRB).

MAN OVERBOARD

Immediate Action
01

Shout "MAN OVERBOARD" and point continuously at the person.

02

Deploy life ring or flotation device immediately.

03

Press the MOB button on the GPS chartplotter.

04

Assign one person to maintain visual contact at all times.

05

Execute Williamson Turn or Anderson Turn to recover.

Fire Response

  1. 1. Sound fire alarm system
  2. 2. Muster at designated station
  3. 3. Shut down fuel supply valves
  4. 4. Deploy CO2 suppression if in engine room

Abandon Ship

  1. 1. Broadcast MAYDAY sequence
  2. 2. Secure EPIRB and grab bags
  3. 3. Launch life rafts from deck
  4. 4. Board rafts with survival gear

Medical Emergency

  1. 1. Assess CAB (Circulation, Airway, Breathing)
  2. 2. Contact Medico Tele-support
  3. 3. Prepare triage area in main salon
  4. 4. Document vital signs every 15 mins
